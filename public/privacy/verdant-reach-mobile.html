Privacy Policy for Verdant Reach

App: Verdant Reach (Android; package com.minormending.verdantreach)
Publisher: Standard Mending Co. (Kevin Ramdath)
Contact: kkramdath@gmail.com
Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

Verdant Reach has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Your game is saved on your own device. The game checks for game updates (new chapters, fixes) from Expo's update service; that check carries a random installation identifier that is not linked to you.

You can optionally sign in with Google to keep a copy of your game in the cloud and continue it on another device or on the web version. Only if you do, we receive your Google account's email address, name and account id, and we store your game save with them. You can sign out at any time and delete that cloud account and save from the game. If you never sign in, we never receive your name, email or game data. We do not sell or share personal information.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your save. Your player name, party, progress, settings and the time of your last session are saved in the app's private storage on your device so the game can continue where you left off and work while you are away. This data stays on your device; the update check described below does not include it. You can erase it at any time with Options > Delete save, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Your player name is whatever you type at the start of the game. It is used only inside the game and stays in your save.
  • Device backups. Your phone's operating system may include an app's private data in its own backup (for example Android's backup to your Google account, if you have it switched on). That is handled by your device and its maker under your device settings and their policies, not by Verdant Reach. Turning device backup off in your settings, or deleting the save in the app, stops or removes it.

Cloud save (optional, only if you sign in)

Signing in is never required: every part of the game works without it.

  • Signing in. Options > Cloud save > Sign in with Google opens Google's own sign-in page in your browser. We ask Google only for your basic profile: your email address, name and Google's account id for you. We never see your Google password, and we get no access to your Google Drive, contacts or anything else.
  • What we store. Your email address, name and account id; a copy of your game save (the same data described under "What the app stores on your device": player name, party, progress, settings and last-session time); when it was last saved; and a sign-in token for each device you are signed in on (stored only as a one-way hash). This is used only to keep your game in step across your devices. It is not used for advertising, analytics or marketing, and we do not send you email.
  • Workshop edits. If you use the Workshop (https://verdant-reach.pages.dev/workshop/) to change plants and trainers, we store your edits (the names, text and numbers you typed) with your account, so your game can use them on every device you sign in on. Only your own game uses them; nobody else sees them. Reset all my edits in the Workshop removes them.
  • Where it is stored. On Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database), which runs the cloud-save service for us as our service provider. Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. Data travels over HTTPS. Like any internet request, requests to the service reveal your IP address to Cloudflare; we do not store it.
  • On your device. The sign-in token and the cloud save's revision number are kept in the app's private storage (or the browser's storage on the web version) until you sign out.
  • How long. Until you delete it. Sign-in tokens expire after a year without use.
  • Deleting it. In the game: Options > Cloud save > Delete cloud account removes your account, every sign-in and your cloud save and Workshop edits from our service immediately (the save on your device stays until you delete it there). You can also do this from the web version at https://verdant-reach.pages.dev, or email kkramdath@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days. Sign out alone keeps the cloud save so you can sign in again later.

Notifications

Verdant Reach can show reminders: Sunlight is full, an expedition is back, the Greenhouse has news, or your party is healed. They are local notifications: the game schedules them on your device, using times it works out from your own save, and your phone shows them when the time comes. No notification server is involved and nothing is sent to you or from you over the internet. The game asks for notification permission only when you turn alerts on, and you can change or revoke it at any time in the app's Options (alerts, quiet hours) or in your phone's settings.

Network use and links

  • App updates (the only network use of the game itself). Verdant Reach can fetch small update bundles (the game's content, code and art) so that new chapters and fixes reach you without a new version from the store. It checks when the app starts and when you return to it, and downloads an update in the background if there is one. The service that provides them is Expo's update service (EAS Update, at u.expo.dev, with the file hosts it points to), run by Expo. Each check or download is an ordinary HTTPS request. It carries:
    • the platform (Android);
    • the app's runtime version (an identifier of the app's native build, not of you) and the update channel it follows;
    • the identifiers of the update that is running, the one built into the app, and any recent updates that failed to start;
    • if the app failed to start last time, a short technical error message about that failure, so that a bad update can be rolled back;
    • a random installation identifier (see below); and
    • what any internet request reveals: your IP address and the device's user agent.

    It contains no name, email, advertising ID, location, contacts, notification token or game data (your save is never sent), and we do not use it to identify or track you.

  • The installation identifier. The update software creates a random identifier (a UUID) the first time it runs and keeps it in the app's private storage for as long as the app stays installed. Expo uses it to count how many installations receive an update and to keep the update service working. It is not derived from your device, your accounts or your advertising ID, and it is not linked to your name or any other detail about you; we cannot tell who it belongs to. Uninstalling the app removes it, and a new one is created if you install again. (If Android's own device backup restores the app's data to a phone, the identifier may be restored with it. Clearing the app's storage removes it.)
  • Expo is our service provider. Expo receives these requests on our behalf, only to deliver updates to the app. We do not use the identifier for advertising or analytics and we do not give it to anyone else. How Expo handles the requests it receives is described in Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy.
  • Cloud save (only if you sign in). See "Cloud save" above. While you are signed in, the game sends your save to the cloud-save service and fetches it from there, when you open or leave the game and after you play.
  • No other network use. The game contacts no other server: no advertising or analytics code, no crash-reporting service, and no push-notification service. Its art, fonts, sounds and the chapters you have installed are on your device, and you can play offline. Updates to the app itself (a new store version) are delivered by Google Play.
  • Options > About has a link to the web version of the game (minormending.github.io/verdant-reach). It opens in your phone's browser only if you tap it. That site is separate, and its host (GitHub) and your browser have their own privacy practices.

If we add another feature that uses the network, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Permissions

The app asks your phone for only what the features above need: showing notifications, and vibrating for haptic feedback (which you can switch off in Options). It uses the internet connection for the update check above and, only if you sign in, for cloud save. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID.

Children

The game's content is mild (cartoon plant battles, no blood, no chat, no user-to-user contact). It asks for no personal information to play; only the optional cloud-save sign-in receives an email address and name. It is not directed to children under 13 (see the store listing for the age range it is aimed at). The one identifier it sends, the random installation identifier used by the update check, is not linked to a person and is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking. If you are a parent and have a question, or believe a child under 13 has used the app, write to kkramdath@gmail.com. To remove a child's game data from the device, use Options > Delete save or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

If you never signed in, we hold no account, name, email or game data about you, so there is nothing of that kind to access, correct or export. Your save is yours, on your device, and Options > Delete save or uninstalling removes it. If you signed in, you can see what we hold (your email and name in Options > Cloud save; your save is your game), delete it as described under "Cloud save", or email kkramdath@gmail.com for a copy or for deletion. The random installation identifier is stored on your device and removed when you uninstall the app (or clear its storage); it is not linked to you, so we cannot look it up for a request, and any copy of the update requests that Expo keeps is governed by Expo's privacy policy. If you do not want the app to contact the update service, you can uninstall it, or use it with the internet turned off for the app in your phone's settings; the game itself works offline. For any privacy question, write to kkramdath@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed, and the change will be described in the release notes of the version that introduces it.
