# Verdant Reach - art credits

## Creature sprites

Every Quickened sprite is traced from a real photograph of the plant by the
photo -> GBC sprite pipeline (`creature-sprites/tool/spritify.py`), then reduced
to 4 colours. Back views and party icons are derived from the same cut-outs
(`tools/art/species.py`). The photographers below made them possible; each
photo is used under the licence shown (public domain, CC0 or CC BY - no

### Notes on stand-in species

- **oak_acorn** (oak/baby.jpg): WHITE OAK (Quercus alba), not robur. Right-hand capped acorn on plain blue studio background, crop on 1280px thumb. 1cm scale bar/label in lower-left corner painted over with background blue (rect 0,742-170,795 in crop coords).
- **bramble_berry** (bramble/teen.jpg): Rubus (blackberry, identified to genus; Austria). Two unripe red berries and one ripening black berry hanging from their stalk, dark background. Red rather than green unripe stage, but it is the cleanest unripe cluster found.
- **fern_fiddlehead** (fern/baby.jpg): Single tightly coiled fiddlehead with stalk, dark green bokeh background. Species not stated (WV; hairy, likely not ostrich fern).
- **sundew_rosette** (sundew/baby.jpg): Drosera aliciae (not rotundifolia): single flat rosette from above, green spoon leaves fringed with red tentacles, Botanischer Garten Berlin-Dahlem. Picked over round-leaved D. rotundifolia because wild rotundifolia photos are tangled in moss and trace as noise; this one gives a clean star rosette in the same red/green as the adult.
- **maple_sapling** (maple/teen.jpg): Acer rubrum (red maple) seedling/sapling per Flickr title, NOT sugar maple. Small young maple with ~15 three-lobed leaves on a single stem, whole plant in frame, growing among grey slate rocks. Yellow-green leaves with some red tinge. Chosen because it reads as a little tree at 48px; no reuse-licensed whole sugar-maple sapling with a clean silhouette was found.
- **sugar_maple** (maple/adult.jpg): Flickr "Lone Tree", Florence/Omaha, Nebraska, tagged maple; species not stated (Acer sp., red-orange autumn crown, could be sugar or red maple). Lone round-crowned tree in a mown field with overcast sky; full crown + trunk in frame and well separated from background treeline. Chosen over the confirmed sugar maples (adult_alt/_alt2) because those crowns reach almost to the ground and the trunk is lost at 56px.
- **moonflower_seed** (moonflower/baby.jpg): Full 1920x1440 download. RELATED SPECIES: Ipomoea tuboides (Hawaiian moon flower, a white night-blooming Ipomoea close to I. alba), no reuse-licensed I. alba seedling photo found. Pair of notched butterfly-shaped cotyledons from above on red soil; reads as a two-leaf seedling.

### Pumpkin, fern, flytrap, sundew, maple, nettle and moonflower lines

These 19 species (pumpkin_blossom to moonflower in the table above) are no
longer traced. Their front, back and icon sprites are original hand-pixeled
art, built shape by shape and pixel by pixel in `tools/art/species_b/`. The
photos listed above were used only as reference for each plant's form and
colour, and their photographers are credited for that.

## Everything else

Tiles, buildings, overworld characters, trainer portraits, item icons, the
title screen, logo, pods and Pressed Marks are original pixel art made for
this game with the generators in `tools/art/` (hand-authored pixel maps and
parametric templates). No third-party art is used for them.
