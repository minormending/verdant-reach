## 1. Beats (the Elite Four and Champion)

1. **The Council Hall opens** after `ch10_done`. Its lobby has a Greenhouse
   counter (a heal point) and a market counter.
2. **The run.** Four seats in sequence, then the Keeper.
   - Seat rooms: `council_1` APOTHECARY (BELLADONNA), `council_2` SLEEPER
     (MIMI OSA), `council_3` ROTTER (TITUS ARUM), `council_4` KINDLER (PYRA).
   - Then `keeper_hall`: **ROWAN VALE**.
   - **The run rule** (Crystal's): once the player enters `council_1`, the
     door behind closes. Each room's exit opens only after its seat is
     beaten.
     - **Losing** whites out to the lobby and **resets the run**: clear the
       four `beat_council_*` flags (and the Keeper's).
     - Leaving any other way isn't possible: there's no return warp
       mid-run.
   - Beating the Keeper sets `beat_keeper`.
3. **The ending** (`ch11_ending`, after the Keeper).
   - DR. VALE (Imogen) arrives, and ROWAN and IMOGEN reconcile (TODO lines).
   - The player becomes a **Fellow of the Herbarium**: the Field Herbarium
     is "the record of a new world".
   - The **credits** roll: a new `credits` script op if none exists. A
     scrolling list of the cast from a data array in `src/world/credits.ts`,
     using the text renderer, skippable with B.
   - Then the **post-game** begins: the player wakes in Fallowfield
     (`player_home`), with `game_cleared` set. The Hall of Fame records the
     team: save the party species and levels under `state.hallOfFame`. This
     is a new optional field: migrate saves.
4. There is no `endSlice` after the credits. The game continues into the
   post-game (POSTGAME.md).

### Amendment (balance)

The lead amends §4 after the wave-3 audit:

1. **The run sim heals between seats.** Players heal from the bag between
   Council rooms, as in Crystal.
   - `simulateTrainerRun` restores HP, status and PP fully **between**
     fights. There's no healing inside a fight.
   - The run must exceed **20% for every starter** with both party cores.
   - Un-skip the run test.
2. **Seat tuning rules:** levels within **±4** of the table; moves free,
   from each species' learnset; and **species swaps only within the seat's
   theme list**:
   - **APOTHECARY** (BELLADONNA): foxglove, oleander, nightshade,
     nightshade_sprout, stinging_nettle;
   - **SLEEPER** (MIMI OSA): sensitive_plant, prayer_plant, moonflower,
     mimosa_sprout, ghost_pipe (it "sleeps" underground most of the year,
     so it's thematically allowed);
   - **ROTTER** (TITUS ARUM): titan_arum, corpse_leaf, skunk_cabbage,
     pitcher_plant, venus_flytrap, bladderwort, sundew;
   - **KINDLER** (PYRA) and the **KEEPER** (ROWAN): no swaps, levels ±2
     only.
3. **Bands (unchanged):** each seat 60–75%, the Keeper 50–62%, and every
   starter above 25% for each fight. Un-skip the per-seat tests.
4. If a band still can't be reached under these rules, **stop and report**
   the closest numbers. Don't loosen a band or a test.
