# World: the Verdant Reach (working name)

A river valley region. It runs from a salt-marsh estuary in the south, up
through farmland and forest, to alpine peaks in the north. The mountains cast a
rain shadow, so the east side is dry scrub and desert. The west side is
old-growth rainforest. At the centre, sealed in a high valley, is the Elder
Grove.

Biomes double as type territories, so where you are tells you what you'll
meet.

## Towns

Conservatories (gyms) are numbered in the intended order.

| Town | Biome | What's here |
|---|---|---|
| **Fallowfield** | Farmland | Home town. The **Fallowfield Herbarium**, run by Dr. Vale. A fallow field is land resting before replanting, which suits a story about beginnings. |
| **Hedgerow** | Lanes and hedges | Hamlet. A retired botanist lives on the route (the "Mr. Pokémon" errand). |
| **Bramblegate** | Hedgerow and woodland edge | **Conservatory 1** (Wood). The first town with a Greenhouse (healing). |
| **Sugarbush** | Maple forest | A syrup town. Rootstock is tapping Quickened maples for their sap: the Slowpoke Well beat. **Conservatory 2** (Bug, meaning carnivorous plants) sits in the bog behind town. |
| **Glasshouse City** | City under a giant glass conservatory dome | The big city: market, Nursery Garden (day care), the **Root Relay** research station. **Conservatory 3** (Bloom). |
| **Cedarhallow** | Ancient cedar forest | Old shrines in living trees. **The Burnt Stand** is a fire-scarred forest where fire-adapted plants grow (pine cones that only open in fire). **Conservatory 4** (Ghost). |
| **Saltmarsh Harbour** | Estuary, mangroves, seagrass | Port town. The **Lantern Tree** on the point has fallen ill (the lighthouse beat). **Conservatory 5** (Water). |
| **Driftseed Isle** | Volcanic island, cactus scrub | Reached by sea. A medicine for the Lantern Tree grows here. **Conservatory 6** (Thorn). |
| **Larchmere** | Alpine lake | Larches are conifers that drop their needles in autumn. **Bloom Lake** has a strange red bloom: a forced Quickening caused by Rootstock's signal (the Lake of Rage beat). Rootstock has a hideout here. **Conservatory 7** (Frost). |
| **Sanguine Ridge** | Dry cliffs with dragon's blood trees | Ancient dragon trees with red resin. **Conservatory 8** (Dragon). |
| **Thistledown** | Desert edge | Tumbleweed country. One of the Wanderers is first sighted here. |
| **The Council Arboretum** | Gates of the Elder Grove | The Council (Elite Four) and the Keeper (Champion). |

## Key locations

- **The Elder Grove** is a valley of pale aspens that are all one organism.
  It's sealed until the climax. Inside it's eerily quiet, with every trunk
  leaning toward you.
- **The Root Relay** is a university station that listens to the root network
  with buried sensors. In Act 2 Rootstock seizes it to *broadcast* through
  the network.
- **The Seed Vault** is a bunker of frozen seeds under the northern peaks:
  every species, kept just in case. It works as a dungeon and as a story
  object. It opens as a **post-game dungeon** (decision Q8).
- **The Centuryheart's Spire** is on the high slope above Cedarhallow, where
  the bloom stands. After the bloom it's dying, as it would in real life.

## Routes

Routes double as micro-biomes: hedgerow lanes, bog boardwalks, a canopy
walkway, a dune path, a scree slope, a seagrass shallows "surf" route, and a
night meadow where moonflowers only appear after dark. Tall grass is literal:
it's where the Quickened hide.
