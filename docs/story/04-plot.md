# Plot outline

Three acts plus a post-game, paced like Crystal: badges gate progress, and
villain set-pieces sit between them.

## Prologue: the Long Bloom

- Night. The player's first evening shift at the Herbarium.
- Dr. Vale drags the player up to the observation deck to watch the
  Centuryheart flower on the far slope for the first time in a hundred years.
- Gold pollen drifts down the valley. A low hum comes up through the floor,
  which nobody else notices.
- Fade out.

## Act 1: Waking (Conservatories 1–3)

**Ch. 1: The greenhouse moved.**
- Morning. The three seedlings in the Herbarium greenhouse have turned to
  face the door. One of them flinches when Vale reaches for it.
- The player chooses a starter (see 05). Vale hands over the Field Herbarium.
  The job: "Find out how many there are, and where."
- **Errand:** Vale sends the player to Old Fennimore on the Hedgerow route,
  who has phoned about "a seed that won't sit still." He hands over a strange
  seed and asks the player to keep it safe. It's a **Centuryheart seed**. Its
  hatch is held back until the very end of the game.
- **Rival:** back at the Herbarium, a second seedling is gone. A boy was seen
  running off. First rival battle: Bram.

**Ch. 2: Bramblegate.**
- Tutorial routes: tall grass, the first wild Quickened, first pods.
- **Conservatory 1** (Hollis, Wood). His lesson: they choose you.

**Ch. 3: Sugarbush.**
- The maple-syrup town is in trouble. Rootstock grunts have tapped the
  Quickened maples in the sugarbush and are draining their sap to study it.
  The maples are wilting.
- This is the first Rootstock encounter: a dungeon through the tapped grove.
- **Conservatory 2** (Nell Pitcher, carnivorous plants).

**Ch. 4: Glasshouse City.**
- The big city under glass. The Nursery Garden opens (seeds and breeding).
- The Root Relay station is introduced: researchers are trying to listen to
  the network.
- **Conservatory 3** (Flora Vance), the difficulty spike.
- **Rival 2:** Bram has a graft collar on his partner and is winning ugly.

## Act 2: Roots (Conservatories 4–7)

**Ch. 5: Cedarhallow.**
- Ancient cedars and the Burnt Stand.
- **Event:** at the Burnt Stand, a fire-cone opens and shows the player a
  vision of the Elder. This is the first hint that something lives under the
  valley.
- **Conservatory 4** (Morrow, Ghost). Morrow's ghost pipes feed on the root
  network, so Morrow has *heard* the Elder: "It isn't dreaming. It's
  frightened." This is the turn from wonder to mystery.

**Ch. 6: Saltmarsh Harbour → Driftseed Isle.**
- The Lantern Tree on the point is sick, and Captain Reyes won't battle until
  it's well.
- The player sails to Driftseed Isle for a cactus-sap remedy.
- **Conservatory 6** (Brother Saguaro, Thorn), on the island.
- Return and heal the Lantern Tree. **Conservatory 5** (Reyes, Water).
- The Conservatories can be done in either order; Crystal's
  Olivine/Cianwood loop works here too.

**Ch. 7: Larchmere and Bloom Lake.**
- Bloom Lake has turned red: hundreds of plants forced awake and furious.
  At the centre is a **giant red-flowered water lily**, a one-off shiny
  encounter (the red Gyarados beat).
- **The reveal:** the Rootstock hideout under the lakeside lodge. Dr. Calloway
  has been broadcasting a signal into the roots to *force* Quickenings.
- Files in the hideout show the first experiment was months *before* the
  Long Bloom. **Rootstock caused the Quickening.**
- **Conservatory 7** (Signe, Frost).
- Dr. Vale admits she knew Mercer Thorne. They studied together, and she
  turned down his offer to join.

**Ch. 8: The Root Relay.**
- Rootstock seizes the Root Relay in Glasshouse City (the Radio Tower beat).
- Wren reverses the sensors to broadcast Mercer's "command" through the
  whole network. Every Quickened plant in the region hears a voice telling it
  to be still.
- The player fights through, floor by floor. Bram arrives, shaken. He read
  the Bloom Lake files too, and he confirms it: his father started the
  Quickening on purpose.
- Defeat Wren. The broadcast stops, but Mercer escapes with the Relay's map
  of the network's hubs.

## Act 3: Bloom (Conservatory 8 → Council)

**Ch. 9: Sanguine Ridge.**
- **Conservatory 8** (Valerian Rook, Dragon).
- Rook tells the player that the Centuryheart is dying, as it must. Its seed
  carries the only "voice" that can calm the Elder. Mercer knows this too.

**Ch. 10: The Elder Grove.**
- Using the stolen hub map, Mercer breaches the sealed valley to graft the
  Elder onto his rootstock.
- The player and Bram go in together.
- The dungeon is a forest that is one creature: paths shift and trunks lean.
- **Climax:** Mercer at the heart, grafting.
  1. Beat Mercer.
  2. Plant the Centuryheart seed the player has carried since Chapter 1. It
     finally sprouts, and the Elder quiets.
  3. **Legendary encounter**: the Elder (see 05).
- The Quickened stay awake. The alarm becomes a choice: they *stay* awake
  because they want to.

**Ch. 11: The Council.**
- Four seats, then the Keeper, Rowan Vale, who reconciles with Imogen.
- Ending: the player becomes a Fellow of the Herbarium, and the Field
  Herbarium is the record of a new world.

## Post-game

- **The Three Wanderers** (roaming legendaries) are released: tumbleweed,
  coconut and burr.
- **Methuselah Ridge** (the mythical): the oldest tree, with time-themed
  events.
- **The Seed Vault** opens as a dungeon.
- **Rematches**, and Mercer in a quiet epilogue in a prison garden, learning
  to tend one plant.
- **The Far Shore**, a Kanto-style second region, is **deferred** until the
  main game exists (decision Q10).
