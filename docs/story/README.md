# Story bible (draft)

> **Pitch.** You're a junior botanist at a regional herbarium. On the night a
> once-a-century flower finally blooms, certain plants across the region
> wake up. They move, they choose, and they defend themselves. Your job is to
> catalogue the Quickened. Then you find out why they woke, and who is trying
> to make every one of them obey.

Every creature is a real plant, and each one evolves through its real life
cycle (seed → sprout → bloom, acorn → sapling → oak). The fantasy is that
they're awake. The botany stays true.

## Structure at a glance

The structure deliberately follows Crystal, so the plot leaves room for the
mechanics:

| Crystal | This game |
|---|---|
| Professor Elm's lab | The Fallowfield Herbarium |
| Pokémon / Pokédex | Quickened / Field Herbarium |
| 8 Gyms → badges | 8 Conservatories → Pressed Marks |
| Team Rocket | Rootstock |
| Slowpoke Well | The sugarbush, where Rootstock taps Quickened maples |
| Radio Tower takeover | The Root Relay takeover (broadcasting through the root network) |
| Ho-Oh / Lugia | The Centuryheart / the Elder Grove |
| Legendary beasts (roaming) | The Three Wanderers: tumbleweed, coconut, burr |
| Celebi | Methuselah, the oldest living pine |
| Elite Four + Champion | The Council + the Keeper |
| Kanto post-game | The Far Shore (deferred) |

- Names are the real plant name plus a stage word.
- About 100 species for v1.
