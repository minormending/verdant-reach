**The lead's decisions** (the owner delegated design through the post-game).
Implementers build exactly this; anything not decided here is an
implementation choice. If something is impossible or contradictory, stop and

## 1. Beats (the bible's climax)

1. **The road west.**
   - After `ch9_done`, Sanguine Ridge's west exit opens.
   - **Route 12** crosses a broken plateau of gorges. It is the Victory
     Road of this game: **3 `root_gap`s on the required path** (ROOT
     BRIDGE), one `pit` row (BOULDER PITS) and the strongest route
     trainers.
   - The exit requires all **8 marks**. Gate NPC `marks_warden` checks them
     (TODO line, then `movePlayer` back).
2. **The Council Arboretum** is the gates of the sealed Elder Grove: the
   Council hall, a Greenhouse and the great Grove Gate.
   - Rootstock grunts hold the gate. The Council hall is shut (door trigger
     `ch10_council_door`: "the Council has gone to the Grove", TODO).
   - **BRAM** arrives (`ch10_bram_joins`). From here he walks with the
     player as a **second follower** if the engine supports two; otherwise
     he appears scripted at each ring (§3).
   - The Grove Gate stands open: Mercer breached it with the hub map.
3. **The Elder Grove, three rings and the heart.** It's a forest that is one
   creature: pale trunks, every one leaning toward the player.
   - **Ring 1** (`elder_grove_1`): SHEARS, the pruning admin from Chapter 3,
     is rematched.
   - **Ring 2** (`elder_grove_2`): **the shifting paths.** Stepping on a
     *listening clearing* toggles `grove_lean`, and the trunks swap which
     lanes are open (`legendWhen`). DR. CALLOWAY waits beyond.
   - **Ring 3** (`elder_grove_3`): WREN.
   - Before the heart, BRAM heals the party (`heal`) and says he'll hold the
     ring.
4. **The heart** (`elder_grove_heart`).
   - MERCER THORNE is grafting a scion onto the Elder's root. ROWAN VALE,
     the Keeper, is held at the edge by grunts.
   - **Battle `mercer`.**
   - Then the planting (`ch10_planting`):
     - `takeItem centuryheart_seed`;
     - the still `centuryheart_sprouts` (stand-in `bloom`);
     - the seed sprouts in the Elder's root, and the alarm quiets.
     - **The Quickened stay awake**: the narration makes clear they *choose*
       to.
   - Mercer is broken but not killed off. ROWAN and BRAM take him out of
     the Grove (hidden).
5. **The Elder** (legendary).
   - Its trunk stays at the heart (NPC `the_elder`).
   - Talking to it starts `wildBattle elder 60` (`canLose: true`).
   - If it's caught, set `elder_caught`. If it's fled or wilted, it stays
     (`elder_caught` false) and can be tried again. **It isn't one-off**:
     the player must not lose the game's legendary to a bad roll.
6. **Chapter end.**
   - Back at the Arboretum (`ch10_end`, `onEnter` when `beat_mercer && 
     !ch10_done`), ROWAN reopens the Council: "come when you're ready".
   - Set `ch10_done` and `slice_done`, then `endSlice`.

- **Bram as a second follower:** only if the follower system can show two
  followers with a small change. Otherwise **don't build it**: Bram appears
  as a scripted NPC at the Arboretum, between rings (`bram_ring_1..3`,
  `visibleWhen` per ring) and before the heart. Say which in your summary.
- **The shifting grove** (ring 2):
  - Two or three listening-clearing **step triggers** each toggle
    `grove_lean` (`setFlag` with the negated value, through an `if`).
  - `legendWhen` swaps `oldgrowth_tree` (or `tree`) and walkable tiles in
    two lane sets.
  - **Puzzle test:** a BFS over (position, `grove_lean`) from the entrance
    reaches Calloway and the exit, and no reachable state soft-locks
    (the entrance is always reachable).
- **Mark check:** a script condition for "has all 8 marks". If none exists,
  add an `ifMarks` op, or check the 8 `beat_<leader>` flags. Say which.

## 9. Decisions taken by the lead

1. **Route 12 is the Victory Road** (ROOT BRIDGE, UPROOT, PRUNE and RAFT, plus 8 marks;
   GLOW and SEED GLIDE are not needed on an open plateau), before the
   Grove rather than after it.
2. **The three admins are rematched**, one per ring, the bible's "dark
   mirrors", before Mercer.
3. **Mercer's team is grafting's classic plants** (apple, rose), and his ace
   is an aspen: he's trying to own the Elder's clone.
4. **The Elder isn't one-off:** a failed catch keeps it at the heart.
5. **The Quickened stay awake by choice:** the planting quiets the alarm
   without putting anyone back to sleep.

## Amendment (lead, after the e2e): losing to the Elder

A loss to the Elder **heals the party in place** ("its roots mend your team")
instead of whiting out. The Elder isn't hostile. Keeping the player at the
heart means a retry is immediate. It also avoids a whiteout to the
Arboretum, which ended Chapter 10 before the Elder was settled. This
supersedes the whiteout resolution of BUGHUNT_CH10 finding 1. The engine
rule that a battle with no healthy party is a loss stands.
